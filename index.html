Privacy Policy

Tap Focus does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email. Scores, coins and settings are stored only on your device. Game Center scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), coarse location, ad interaction and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. In the EEA/UK a consent form (Google UMP) is shown, and you can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings > Privacy Settings. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt or in iOS Settings. See how Google uses data.

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Popsling: Chain & Pop

Chain same-colored balls, pull back like a slingshot and pop the jar. 2000 levels, powers and fiestas.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Popsling does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email. Your progress, lives, powers and settings are stored on your device. On iOS, progress may also be synced through your own iCloud account (Apple's privacy policy applies); we cannot see this data.

The app shows optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID; on iOS only if you allow tracking), coarse location, ad interaction and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. In the EEA/UK a consent form (Google UMP) is shown, and you can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings > Privacy Settings. See how Google uses data.

The app may use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (for example which levels are started, won or lost) to improve game balance. This data is not used to identify you.

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your lives are full). No notification data leaves your device.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

You can delete all data by uninstalling the app.

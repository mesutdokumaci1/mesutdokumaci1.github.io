Lock Pick: Tap Master

Spin, tap, crack the lock. A one-tap timing game with a Daily Challenge, leaderboards and unlockable neon themes.

Privacy Policy

Lock Pick does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email. Scores, coins and settings are stored only on your device. Leaderboard scores are handled by Apple Game Center or Google Play Games under their own privacy policies.

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, on iOS only if you allow tracking), coarse location, ad interaction and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. In the EEA/UK a consent form (Google UMP) is shown, and you can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings > Privacy Settings. See how Google uses data.

The app is not directed at children under 13.
